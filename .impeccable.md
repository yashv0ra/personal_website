## Design Context

### Users
Visitors exploring Yash Vora's portfolio, background, resume, and experiments. Existing resume/chat support recruiters and collaborators.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, tactile, suspenseful. User requests a movie entrance and realistic Three.js room, with no jump scares or autoplay audio.

### Aesthetic Direction
Pure white early-internet button; expand and fade to black; dolly into a concrete room with a hanging practical lamp. One square card with an emblem and title. On navigation: lamp off, physical card exchange, restrained ignition. About, Resume, Lab.

### Design Principles
- Make light sources and materials believable.
- Restrain text; let the sequence communicate the experience.
- Cinematic entrance, shorter repeated navigation.
- Accessible HTML actions, reduced motion, rendering fallback.
- Inspect browser renders and recordings, then iterate.
